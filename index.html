Shortlist

A job-search service built on one discipline: only apply to jobs a client is genuinely qualified for. This site is a working index of the Phase 1 build — the client feed, the engine that runs the qualification gate and resume tailoring, the data schemas it runs on, and the legal drafts governing the service — not the product itself.
